Setup Guide

How to Add Mojo 4K IPTV to MyTV Online — 5 Steps

  1. 1
    Subscribe to Mojo 4K
    Visit /pricing or start a free 24-hour trial. Your Xtream Codes credentials (server URL, username, password) arrive via WhatsApp in 60 seconds.
  2. 2
    Open MyTV Online on your Formuler
    Power on your Formuler Z8, Z10, Z11, ZX or Z+ Neo and open the MyTV Online or myTVonline2 app from the home screen.
  3. 3
    Go to My Services and Add Service
    In MyTV Online, navigate to My Services (or tap the + icon on newer versions) and select Add Service or Add New Service.
  4. 4
    Select Type: Xtream and enter credentials
    Set the Service Type to Xtream. Enter your Mojo 4K server hostname (without http://), username and password. Tap Save or OK.
  5. 5
    Browse 50,000+ channels with EPG
    MyTV Online loads your full Mojo 4K channel list and EPG automatically. Browse live sport, movies, news, and international channels from your Formuler remote.
