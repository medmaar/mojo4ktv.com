Emby IPTV: Add Live TV to Your Media Server

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Emby Server can treat an IPTV subscription as a TV tuner. You add your M3U link as a tuner and your XMLTV link as the guide, and live channels then appear in every Emby app next to your own movies and shows. This guide covers the setup, guide mapping, stream limits and when Plex or Jellyfin is the better fit.

Adding IPTV to Emby Server

  1. In the Emby dashboard, open Live TV and choose Add TV source.
  2. Select M3U Tuner and paste your M3U link. Set the number of simultaneous streams to match your plan's device count.
  3. Under TV Guide Data Providers, add XMLTV and paste your EPG link.
  4. Map any unmatched channels to guide entries, then refresh guide data.
  5. Open Live TV in any Emby app to watch.

Your Mojo 4K credentials (Xtream Codes login, M3U link and, for MAG-style apps, a portal URL) arrive by WhatsApp or email within minutes of ordering or starting the free trial.

Tips for a Clean Emby Live TV Setup

  • Trim the playlist. A 50,000-channel M3U makes the guide slow. Hide countries and categories you never watch in Emby's channel settings.
  • Stream limits. Each live stream uses one connection on your IPTV plan. A 1-device plan means one live stream through Emby at a time, even across users.
  • Transcoding. Remote viewing may trigger transcoding; a server with hardware transcoding handles 4K far better.
  • Premiere features. Some Live TV features, notably DVR recording, are tied to an Emby Premiere subscription; check Emby's current feature list.
