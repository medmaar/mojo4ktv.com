What is IPTV and How Does it Work?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers live TV channels and on-demand content over your broadband connection — the same internet you use for Netflix and YouTube. Instead of a satellite dish or cable wire, you stream 50,000+ channels directly to any device: Firestick, Smart TV, phone, tablet or PC. No hardware installation required.
