Chromecast with Google TV is one of the best streaming devices for IPTV. Running Android TV, it supports the Google Play Store natively — which means you can install IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate, or any other Android IPTV player directly from the store.

Every Mojo 4K subscription provides Xtream Codes credentials and an M3U URL that work perfectly with both IPTV Smarters Pro and TiviMate on Google TV. Setup takes less than 5 minutes with our WhatsApp support team on standby.
