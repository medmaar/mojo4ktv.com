How to Set Up IPTV on Philips Smart TV

1

Check your Philips TV OS

Identify whether your Philips TV runs Android TV or Philips' own Linux-based system. Android TV Philips (2015+) has Google Play; older models need Smart IPTV.

2

For Android TV Philips: install from Play Store

Open Google Play Store → search 'IPTV Smarters Pro' or 'TiviMate' → install. Enter your Mojo 4K credentials under Xtream Codes.

3

For older Philips (non-Android): use Smart IPTV

Visit the Smart IPTV website (siptv.eu) and register your Philips TV's MAC address. Then use your Mojo 4K M3U URL in Smart IPTV.

4

Or use Chromecast from Phone

Plug a Chromecast or Chromecast with Google TV into your Philips TV's HDMI port. Stream IPTV from your phone to the Chromecast directly.

5

Or use Amazon Firestick

The simplest Philips TV IPTV solution: plug a Firestick 4K into any HDMI port. Install TiviMate, add Mojo 4K credentials, watch 50,000+ channels.

IPTV Philips TV — Frequently Asked Questions

Does IPTV work on Philips Smart TV?
Yes — Philips Android TV models (most 2015+) support IPTV via Google Play apps like TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro. Older Philips Linux-based smart TVs use Smart IPTV or SS IPTV installed via USB or browser.
What IPTV app works on Philips TV?
For Philips Android TV: TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro from Google Play. For older Philips TVs: Smart IPTV, Net IPTV, or SS IPTV. The universal solution for any Philips TV model is a Firestick 4K via HDMI.
How do I find my Philips TV's MAC address for IPTV?
On Philips Android TV: Settings → Network → Wi-Fi or Ethernet → view MAC address. For Smart IPTV on older Philips: the MAC address shows when you launch the Smart IPTV app on the TV.
Can I use Firestick with Philips TV for IPTV?
Yes — plug a Firestick 4K or 4K Max into any HDMI port on your Philips TV. Switch the TV to that HDMI input. The Firestick runs IPTV independently — works on any Philips TV model regardless of smart TV OS.
