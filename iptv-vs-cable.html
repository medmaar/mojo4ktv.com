Cable TV costs £40–100/month in the UK, $80–150/month in the US and CAD $100–200/month in Canada after introductory offers expire. IPTV costs $9–25/month for a comparable or larger channel package. Over a 12-month period, switching to IPTV can save $500–1,500 per household.

Most cable providers offer 200–500 channels. Mojo 4K IPTV provides 50,000+ live channels from 152 countries — including every channel your cable provider carries, plus thousands they don't: international channels, sports from every continent, niche content and language-specific packages.

Cable TV requires an engineer visit, set-top box installation and often a 12–24 month contract. IPTV is set up in under 5 minutes on any device you already own — Firestick, Smart TV, iPhone, iPad, Android or PC. No engineer, no hardware, no contract.
