IPTV Renewal — Common Questions

Does my subscription auto-renew?

No. All Mojo 4K plans are one-time payments with no auto-renewal or recurring billing. When your plan expires, you simply choose whether to renew. You will never be charged automatically.

Can I renew early?

Yes. Renewing before your current plan expires extends your subscription from the current expiry date — you do not lose any remaining time. Contact WhatsApp support for a seamless early renewal.

Can I upgrade when renewing?

Yes. When renewing, you can switch to a longer plan (e.g., from monthly to annual) or add more simultaneous connections (e.g., from 1 device to 3 devices). Contact WhatsApp support to combine plan upgrades with renewal.

What happens if I renew after expiry?

After expiry, your credentials become inactive. Purchase a new plan and fresh credentials arrive within 60 seconds via WhatsApp. Your TiviMate and app settings are saved locally — just enter the new credentials and streaming resumes.

Do I get the same credentials on renewal?

Early renewal (before expiry): your existing credentials are extended and remain identical. Post-expiry renewal: new credentials are issued. Both scenarios result in your apps working normally — just update the new details if credentials change.

Is there a loyalty discount for renewals?

Yes. Existing subscribers often receive loyalty pricing on renewals. Contact our WhatsApp support team before renewing to ask about current subscriber discounts — we appreciate long-term customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my IPTV login change when I renew?+

No. Your server URL, username and password stay exactly the same when you renew. TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, Kodi and all other configured apps continue working without any changes.

Can I upgrade my plan when I renew?+

Yes. When renewing, you can switch to a longer duration (e.g. from 1-month to 12-month) or add more simultaneous connections. Your login credentials remain unchanged; only your subscription duration and connection count change.

What happens if my IPTV subscription expires?+

Your login credentials stop working at midnight on your expiry date. Your TiviMate or Smarters app will show "Authentication failed" or similar. Simply renew your subscription and channels restore within seconds — no reconfiguration required.

How far in advance can I renew?+

You can renew at any time, including weeks before expiry. When you renew early, the new period is added to your existing expiry date — so you never lose any time from your current subscription.
