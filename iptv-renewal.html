Frequently Asked Questions

Does my IPTV login change when I renew?+

No. Your server URL, username and password stay exactly the same when you renew. TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, Kodi and all other configured apps continue working without any changes.

Can I upgrade my plan when I renew?+

Yes. When renewing, you can switch to a longer duration (e.g. from 1-month to 12-month) or add more simultaneous connections. Your login credentials remain unchanged; only your subscription duration and connection count change.

What happens if my IPTV subscription expires?+

Your login credentials stop working at midnight on your expiry date. Your TiviMate or Smarters app will show "Authentication failed" or similar. Simply renew your subscription and channels restore within seconds — no reconfiguration required.

How far in advance can I renew?+

You can renew at any time, including weeks before expiry. When you renew early, the new period is added to your existing expiry date — so you never lose any time from your current subscription.
