What is IPTV? Internet Protocol Television Explained

Last updated: August 9, 2026

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers live TV channels, sports, movies and series directly over your internet connection — replacing the need for a satellite dish or cable box.

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Unlike traditional broadcast TV, cable or satellite — which transmit signals over the airwaves or a physical cable — IPTV delivers television content as data packets over an internet connection (IP network). This allows you to watch live TV, on-demand movies, and sports from anywhere with an internet connection.

How Does IPTV Work?

An IPTV service like Mojo 4K operates a network of high-performance media servers. When you subscribe, you receive login credentials (username, password, and server URL). You enter these into an IPTV player app — such as IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate — on your device. The app connects to the server and streams content directly to your screen in real time.

The content is delivered using the Xtream Codes or M3U playlist protocol, which is the industry standard for IPTV streaming services. Premium providers like Mojo 4K use dedicated, load-balanced server infrastructure to maintain high picture quality and low buffering even when millions of viewers are watching simultaneously.

What is the Difference Between IPTV and Streaming Services?

Services like Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video are on-demand streaming platforms — they don't offer live TV channels or live sports. IPTV gives you everything: 50,000+ live channels (including every sport, every PPV), plus a 300,000+ on-demand library of movies and series — all in one subscription starting from $9/month.

Is IPTV Legal?

IPTV technology itself is completely legal — it is simply a method of delivering video content over IP networks. Many legitimate broadcasters and streaming platforms use IPTV technology. Users are responsible for ensuring the content they access complies with local copyright laws.

What Internet Speed Do I Need for IPTV?

For HD channels you need at least 10 Mbps. For Full HD, 15–20 Mbps. For 4K IPTV channels, 25 Mbps or more is recommended. A stable wired or 5GHz Wi-Fi connection will give the best results.
