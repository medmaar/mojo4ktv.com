Buying IPTV on AliExpress, eBay or Amazon?

Last updated: November 6, 2026

IPTV subscriptions listed on AliExpress, Alibaba, eBay and Amazon are almost always third-party resellers passing on codes from an unknown source. Marketplace protection usually covers delivery of the code, not whether the service keeps working next month. It can work out, but you take on more risk than buying from a provider with a trial, support and a written refund policy.

How Marketplace IPTV Listings Work

A seller buys credits from an IPTV panel, then lists "12 months IPTV" codes. When you order, you receive a username and password or an activation code. The seller is rarely the operator of the servers, so when something breaks, they may not be able to fix it, and the listing can vanish.

Why Buyer Protection Rarely Helps

  • Most protection windows close weeks after delivery, while problems often appear months later.
  • The seller can show the code was "delivered", which satisfies the claim.
  • Listings for IPTV services can be removed by the marketplace, taking seller history with them.

Red Flags in a Listing

  1. 12 months for the price of a coffee.
  2. "Lifetime" or "no monthly fees forever".
  3. No trial, no named service, no support contact outside the marketplace chat.
  4. Boxes sold "fully loaded" with free channels; see IPTV with box.
  5. Reviews that all mention delivery speed and none mention quality after a few months.

A Safer Way to Buy IPTV

  • Test first: use a free trial at prime time.
  • Check the refund policy: Mojo 4K's is a written 14-day money-back guarantee.
  • Talk to support before paying: a provider who answers quickly before the sale will answer after it.
  • Buy hardware separately: a Firestick or Android box from any retailer works with any service; see best IPTV box.
