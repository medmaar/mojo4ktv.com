Setup on TV, Phone or Box

  1. Start the trial or pick a plan and receive your login by WhatsApp or email.
  2. Install a player: TiviMate (Firestick, Android TV), IBO Player or Smart IPTV (Samsung, LG), Smarters Player Lite or IPTVX (iPhone, Apple TV).
  3. Sign in with Xtream Codes, open the Albanian (ALB) category and add channels to favourites.
  4. Adjust the EPG time shift if guide times do not match your local time.

Time Zones

Albania and Kosovo use Central European Time: six hours ahead of Toronto and New York, one hour ahead of London. Prime-time shows from Tirana air in the early afternoon in Eastern Canada, so channels with catch-up and a good TV guide make a real difference.
