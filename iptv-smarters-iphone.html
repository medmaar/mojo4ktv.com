IPTV Smarters on iPhone & iPad: Smarters Player Lite Setup

Last updated: November 7, 2026

On iPhone and iPad, IPTV Smarters is listed in the App Store as Smarters Player Lite. It is free, it holds no channels, and it takes about three minutes to connect to an IPTV subscription using an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link. This guide covers install, login, AirPlay to your TV and the errors iOS users hit most.

Download Smarters Player Lite on iPhone and iPad

Open the App Store and search for Smarters Player Lite. If you search "IPTV Smarters Pro iPhone" you will see several look-alike players; check the developer name and reviews before installing. The same app runs on iPhone, iPad and, through the App Store on tvOS, on Apple TV (see the Apple TV guide).

  • Price: free to download. Your channels come from your IPTV provider.
  • Works with: Xtream Codes API logins and M3U playlists.
  • iOS version: runs on current iOS and iPadOS releases; update iOS first if the App Store says it is incompatible.

Log In with Your IPTV Subscription

  1. Get your login: start a Mojo 4K free 24-hour trial or choose a plan. Details arrive by WhatsApp or email.
  2. Open Smarters Player Lite, accept the terms and tap Login with Xtream Codes API.
  3. Type any name, then the username, password and server URL exactly as sent (include http:// and the port).
  4. Tap Add User. Live TV, movies, series and the guide download in under a minute.
  5. Tap the profile to open the dashboard. Add favourites with a long press on a channel.

Copy and paste the URL from WhatsApp rather than typing it: one wrong character is the most common reason for an "invalid login" message.

Watch on Your TV: AirPlay and Screen Mirroring

Smarters Player Lite supports AirPlay. Start a channel, tap the AirPlay icon in the player and choose your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible Samsung, LG or Sony TV. If the icon is missing, use Screen Mirroring from Control Centre instead; quality is a little lower and your phone must stay unlocked.

For daily TV viewing, a dedicated device is more comfortable: Smarters on Apple TV or a Firestick keep the phone free.

Common iPhone Errors and Fixes

ProblemFix
"Invalid username or password"Paste the credentials again, check there is no space at the end, and confirm the server URL includes the port.
Channels load but guide is emptyOpen Settings › EPG and tap refresh; wait a minute after first login.
Stream stops when the screen locksEnable picture-in-picture in iOS Settings › General, or keep the app in the foreground.
Buffering on mobile dataLower the stream quality or switch to Wi-Fi; see buffering fixes.

Other iPhone IPTV Apps Worth Knowing
