Our Story

Mojo 4K — Built for Cord-Cutters Who Won't Compromise

Mojo 4K was founded in 2020 with a single observation: IPTV technology had matured to the point where streaming quality exceeded cable, but most IPTV providers were still running on unreliable infrastructure with non-existent support. We built Mojo 4K to change that.

Our principle from day one: own the infrastructure. Instead of reselling another provider's stream, Mojo 4K built and operates its own server clusters across multiple data centres. When you contact support, you reach the same team that manages the servers — there is no upstream provider to blame, no chain of resellers to navigate. If something goes wrong, we fix it.

Six years and 100,000+ active subscribers later, that principle still guides every decision. We have never raised our base monthly price. We have maintained 99.97% uptime across the same full library of 50,000+ channels and 300,000+ VOD titles. Our WhatsApp support team responds in under 5 minutes around the clock.
