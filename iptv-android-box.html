IPTV Android Box — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Android TV box for IPTV?
The Nvidia Shield TV Pro is the best Android TV box for IPTV — 4K HDR, Dolby Vision, 16GB storage, and Plex Media Server support. For budget picks, the Xiaomi Mi Box S, Mecool KM2 Plus, or X96 Max are all excellent for IPTV streaming.
Does Mojo 4K work on all Android TV boxes?
Yes — Mojo 4K's Xtream Codes and M3U credentials work on every Android TV box with TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, GSE Smart IPTV, OTT Navigator or any Xtream-compatible player.
How do I install TiviMate on Android box without Google Play?
Use the Downloader app (free on most Android boxes) to download the TiviMate APK directly. Enter the official TiviMate website URL in Downloader, then install the APK file.
Can I get 4K IPTV on an Android box?
Yes — any Android TV box that supports 4K playback (most boxes with H.265/HEVC hardware decoding) can stream 4K IPTV channels. Mojo 4K provides 500+ dedicated 4K streams for 4K-capable devices.
Setup Guide

How to Set Up Mojo 4K IPTV on Android TV Box

  1. 1
    Identify your Android TV box type — Determine if your box runs Android TV OS (Nvidia Shield, Mi Box, Mecool, Chromecast with Google TV) or standard Android (generic Chinese boxes). Android TV OS boxes have Google Play Store and install TiviMate directly. Standard Android boxes may need to sideload APKs.
  2. 2
    Install TiviMate (Android TV OS boxes) — On Android TV OS: open Google Play Store → search 'TiviMate' → install. This is the fastest and cleanest IPTV app for Android TV boxes with remote control navigation.
  3. 3
    Install IPTV Smarters Pro (standard Android boxes) — On standard Android boxes: open Google Play → search 'IPTV Smarters Pro' → install. If Google Play is unavailable on your box, download the APK directly using a browser.
  4. 4
    Enter your Mojo 4K credentials — In TiviMate: Settings → Playlists → Add → Xtream Codes. In IPTV Smarters: Add User → Xtream Codes. Enter your server URL, username and password from your WhatsApp message.
  5. 5
    Optimise for your box's performance — In TiviMate settings, set the player to ExoPlayer for hardware-accelerated decoding. For 4K channels, ensure your box is connected via ethernet rather than WiFi to avoid buffering.

Best Android TV boxes for IPTV: (1) Nvidia Shield Pro — the most powerful, ideal for 4K and multiple streams; (2) Amazon Firestick 4K Max — best value; (3) Mi Box S 4K — affordable mid-range; (4) Mecool KM7 — budget-friendly Android TV option.
