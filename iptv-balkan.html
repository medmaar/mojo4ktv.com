Setup in Four Steps

  1. Start the free trial or choose a plan; credentials arrive by WhatsApp or email.
  2. Install a player: TiviMate on Firestick or Android TV, IBO Player or Smart IPTV on Samsung or LG, IPTVX on Apple TV.
  3. Sign in with Xtream Codes and open the country categories.
  4. Add your channels to a "Balkan" favourites group and set the guide's time shift to your local time.

Time Zones and the TV Guide

Central European Time is six hours ahead of Toronto and nine ahead of Vancouver. Players such as TiviMate show the guide in your device's local time, so evening news from Belgrade or Zagreb appears at lunchtime in Eastern Canada. If times look wrong, adjust the EPG time shift in the app settings. Catch-up, where available on a channel, lets you watch the evening news later.

IPTV Ponuda, IPTV Televizija and IPTV Kanali

Searches such as IPTV ponuda (IPTV offer), IPTV televizija and IPTV kanali (IPTV channels) come from viewers in the Balkan diaspora looking for a package with ex-YU channels. The checks are the same as anywhere: confirm the specific channels you want are in the line-up, test them in the evening with the free trial, and choose a plan length after that (pricing).

More Questions

What does "IPTV ponuda" mean?

It means "IPTV offer" in Serbian, Croatian and Bosnian, and usually refers to a package that includes Balkan channels.
