Why Sports Fans Switch from FuboTV to IPTV

FuboTV was built for sports fans, but it has a critical gap: no PPV events. Every UFC main card ($74.99 on ESPN+), every boxing PPV, and every WWE premium live event requires a separate purchase that FuboTV can't help with. Mojo 4K IPTV includes every PPV event as part of the standard subscription — with no per-event fees. For regular PPV viewers, this alone makes IPTV the more cost-effective choice even before factoring in the $70/month price difference.

FuboTV also lacks the international sports coverage that global fans need. Premier League, Champions League, Bundesliga, Serie A, La Liga, Formula 1, and cricket — all central to Mojo 4K's channel lineup — require expensive add-on packages on FuboTV or aren't available at all. For anyone watching sports from outside the US system, IPTV provides dramatically more value.
