IPTV Activation — Common Questions

How long does activation take?

Credentials are sent via WhatsApp within 60 seconds of payment. If you have not received them within 5 minutes, check your WhatsApp number is correct and message our 24/7 support team directly.

What if my credentials do not work?

The most common cause is a copy-paste error with extra spaces. Enter the server URL, username and password manually. If the issue persists, contact 24/7 WhatsApp support — we resolve credential issues in minutes.

When does my subscription start?

Your subscription period starts from the moment your credentials are sent, not when you first connect. A 30-day subscription from August 16 expires on September 15 regardless of when you first log in.

Can I activate on multiple devices?

Yes. Enter the same credentials on multiple devices up to your plan's connection limit (1-5 simultaneous streams). Each device can stream a different channel simultaneously — no separate activation required per device.
