How to Add IPTV to Stremio

Stremio is a popular media player available on Windows, Mac, Android, iOS, Android TV, and Firestick. While Stremio focuses primarily on on-demand content and add-ons, you can add IPTV live TV channels to Stremio using the IPTV Channels community add-on and an M3U playlist.

Every Mojo 4K subscription includes an M3U playlist URL that works with compatible Stremio add-ons for live TV. Simply install the IPTV add-on in Stremio and enter your M3U URL to access your channels.

How to Use Mojo 4K IPTV with Stremio

Stremio is an add-on based streaming platform. IPTV channels can be added to Stremio via community add-ons that accept M3U playlist URLs. Mojo 4K's M3U_plus URL integrates into Stremio's live channel section — giving you 50,000+ channels within the Stremio interface alongside streaming add-ons.

  1. 1
    Subscribe to Mojo 4K — Get your M3U URL at mojo4ktv.com/pricing. Your URL arrives via WhatsApp in 60 seconds. No credit card for the free trial.
  2. 2
    Open Stremio and go to Add-ons — Click the puzzle piece icon (Add-ons) in the top bar of Stremio.
  3. 3
    Search for an IPTV add-on — Search 'IPTV' in the Stremio add-on catalogue. Community add-ons like 'IPTV' or 'External Player' accept M3U URLs.
  4. 4
    Configure with your M3U URL — Enter your Mojo 4K M3U_plus URL when the add-on prompts for a playlist URL. The add-on imports all 50,000+ channels.
  5. 5
    Access channels in Stremio Board — Your Mojo 4K channels appear in the Stremio board under the add-on. Browse by category or search for specific channels.

Note: Stremio's IPTV integration via community add-ons works but provides less EPG accuracy than dedicated IPTV apps. For the best experience with full 7-day EPG, TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro are recommended.
