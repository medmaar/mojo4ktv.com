TiviMate Premium IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

What is TiviMate Premium?
TiviMate Premium is the paid unlock for the TiviMate IPTV player app. It costs approximately $4.99/year and unlocks features including the EPG guide, multi-panel view, catch-up TV, and DVR recording. The base TiviMate app is free.
Do I need TiviMate Premium for Mojo 4K?
No — Mojo 4K works with both free and Premium TiviMate. However, TiviMate Premium is highly recommended as it unlocks the EPG guide which shows what's on all 50,000+ channels.
What is the TiviMate Premium price?
TiviMate Premium costs approximately $4.99/year (prices may vary by region). This is purchased as an in-app unlock via Google Play. This is separate from your Mojo 4K IPTV subscription.
How do I create a TiviMate account?
TiviMate Premium is linked to your Google Play account — there's no separate TiviMate account to create. Purchase TiviMate Premium from any device with your Google account and it unlocks on all your Android/Firestick devices.
Is TiviMate better than IPTV Smarters Pro?
TiviMate is generally considered the best IPTV player for Android TV and Firestick, especially with Premium. It has a superior UI, better EPG, and smoother performance. IPTV Smarters Pro is a great free alternative.
