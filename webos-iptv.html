IPTV on LG webOS Smart TV

LG webOS is the operating system on LG Smart TVs. While webOS doesn't support all IPTV apps, it has excellent native IPTV support through apps available in the LG Content Store, plus full compatibility via a connected streaming device.

The best native webOS IPTV apps are Smart IPTV and SS IPTV — both available in the LG Content Store, both supporting M3U playlists that work directly with your Mojo 4K subscription.

For LG OLED and QLED TV owners who want the best IPTV experience, connecting a Firestick 4K Max via HDMI is highly recommended. This gives you access to TiviMate Premium with full EPG, catch-up TV, DVR recording, and native 4K — a major step up from webOS native apps.
