What Is an IPTV Service?

An IPTV service (Internet Protocol Television service) delivers live television, on-demand movies, and series over your internet connection instead of through cable, satellite, or antenna. You subscribe to an IPTV service the same way you subscribe to Netflix — except instead of on-demand content only, you get 50,000+ live TV channels from around the world.

How an IPTV Service Works

When you subscribe to an IPTV service like Mojo 4K, you receive a set of credentials: a server URL, username, and password (Xtream Codes format) plus an M3U playlist URL. You enter these credentials into an IPTV player app on your device. The player connects to the IPTV service's server and streams live channels to your screen in real-time — exactly like a cable TV signal, but delivered over internet bandwidth instead of coaxial cable.
