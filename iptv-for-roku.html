Setup Guide

How to Get IPTV on Roku — What Actually Works in 2026

  1. 1
    Understand Roku's IPTV limitation — Roku does not natively support Xtream Codes or M3U IPTV apps — the Roku Channel Store does not carry TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro. The workaround: use Roku's built-in screen mirroring (Android) or connect a dedicated IPTV device alongside your Roku.
  2. 2
    Method 1: Screen mirror from Android phone — On your Android phone, install IPTV Smarters Pro and enter your Mojo 4K Xtream Codes credentials. Pull down the notification panel and enable Screen Cast / Smart View. Select your Roku device. Your phone streams Mojo 4K channels on your TV via Roku without any Roku channel installation.
  3. 3
    Method 2: Private channel Roku IPTV apps — Some IPTV player apps are available as Roku private channels (not listed in the public Channel Store). Add them via your Roku account at my.roku.com/account/add using the channel's access code. These apps accept M3U playlist URLs from Mojo 4K.
  4. 4
    Method 3: Upgrade to a better IPTV device — For the best IPTV experience, consider replacing or supplementing your Roku with a Firestick 4K Max or Chromecast with Google TV. Both support TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro natively. The Firestick 4K Max costs £55 / $60 and provides full 4K IPTV without workarounds.
  5. 5
    Get your Mojo 4K M3U URL for Roku-compatible apps — Subscribe at /pricing or start a free trial. Your M3U_plus URL and Xtream Codes credentials arrive via WhatsApp in 60 seconds. Use the M3U URL for Roku-compatible channel apps, and Xtream Codes for IPTV Smarters Pro on your phone.

Roku's closed ecosystem makes it the least IPTV-friendly major streaming platform. The Firestick 4K Max is the recommended upgrade — same price range, full native IPTV support.
