Lazy IPTV & Lazy IPTV Deluxe: Android Setup

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Lazy IPTV is a lightweight Android IPTV player built around playlists, favourites and viewing history. Lazy IPTV Deluxe is the newer version with a TV-friendly interface. Both are free to start, both need your own playlist, and both are good choices on low-powered phones and older Android boxes.

Features and pricing are set by the developer and change between releases, so treat the table as a guide and check the version you install.

Adding Your Playlist

  1. Install Lazy IPTV Deluxe (or the classic app) from Google Play.
  2. Tap Add playlist and choose From URL.
  3. Paste your M3U link. If your version offers a provider or Xtream login, you can use your username, password and server URL instead.
  4. Open settings and add the EPG (XMLTV) link so the guide fills in.
  5. Long-press channels to add them to favourites for quick access.

Your Mojo 4K credentials (Xtream Codes login, M3U link and, for MAG-style apps, a portal URL) arrive by WhatsApp or email within minutes of ordering or starting the free trial.

Tips for a Smoother Lazy IPTV Experience

  • Use an external player such as VLC or MX Player if a channel will not start in the built-in one.
  • Keep one playlist per provider; duplicate playlists slow down loading on older phones.
  • On a TV box, the Deluxe layout is much easier with a remote than the classic phone layout.

When to Choose Another Player

Lazy IPTV is about simplicity. If you want catch-up, a full movie library view or recording, look at TiviMate on Android TV or IPTV Smarters on Android. For a free, equally simple alternative, try Purple Player.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lazy IPTV is free to download and use, with optional paid extras depending on the version. It does not include channels; you add your own playlist.

Deluxe is the newer app with a TV-friendly interface and a guide grid, better suited to Android TV boxes. The classic app is lighter and phone-focused.

It is an Android app, so it can be sideloaded on Fire TV, but its layout is designed for touch. On Firestick, TiviMate or IPTV Smarters is easier to use.

Open the app settings, find the EPG or TV guide section and paste the XMLTV link from your provider, then refresh.
