IPTV Player — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best IPTV player?

TiviMate is the best IPTV player for Firestick and Android TV. IPTV Smarters Pro is the best cross-platform player for all other devices. Both accept Mojo 4K Xtream Codes credentials and provide full 7-day EPG data.

What is the difference between an IPTV player and an IPTV service?

An IPTV player (TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro) is the free app on your device that displays channels. An IPTV service (Mojo 4K) is the subscription providing the channels and server infrastructure. You need both — the player is free, the subscription is from $9/month.

Which IPTV player works on all devices?

IPTV Smarters Pro works on Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, and Apple TV — making it the most device-universal IPTV player. Your Mojo 4K credentials work simultaneously on multiple devices up to your plan's limit.
