What is the Smart IPTV App?

Smart IPTV (also known as SIPTV) is a popular IPTV player application for Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen) and LG Smart TVs (webOS). It supports M3U playlists, Xtream Codes, and XMLTV EPG guides — making it one of the most widely used apps for native Smart TV IPTV.

To use Smart IPTV, you need an active IPTV subscription that provides an M3U URL or Xtream Codes credentials. Mojo 4K provides both with every subscription, starting from just $9/month. The activation process takes under 5 minutes.

SS IPTV is an alternative Smart TV IPTV app available on Samsung and LG TVs. Both Smart IPTV and SS IPTV work with your Mojo 4K M3U URL, giving you access to 50,000+ live channels in HD and 4K directly on your Smart TV.

FAQ — Smart IPTV App

Both Smart IPTV (siptv.eu) and SS IPTV are M3U player apps for Samsung and LG Smart TVs. Smart IPTV has been around longer and has wider Smart TV compatibility. SS IPTV offers more free activations per device. Both work with your Mojo 4K M3U URL.

Install Smart IPTV on your Samsung or LG TV. Open it and note the device MAC address. On a computer, go to siptv.eu/mylist, enter the MAC address and your Mojo 4K M3U URL, then reload the app on your TV.
