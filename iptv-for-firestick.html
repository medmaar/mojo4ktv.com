How to Sideload IPTV Apps on Firestick (APK Method)

TiviMate and several premium IPTV apps aren't available on the Amazon App Store but can be sideloaded onto any Fire TV Stick using an APK file. Enable developer options first: go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → turn on Apps from Unknown Sources. Then install the Downloader app (free on the App Store), open it, and paste the TiviMate APK URL to download and install directly.

For Kodi on Firestick, the same process applies. Download the Kodi APK from the official kodi.tv website via Downloader, install it, then add the IPTV Simple Client PVR add-on from Kodi's official repository. Configure it with your Mojo 4K M3U playlist URL and restart — your channels appear under Live TV in the Kodi menu. Kodi is free and fully open-source.

The developer options method works on all Fire TV Stick models — Fire Stick Lite, Fire Stick 4K, Fire Stick 4K Max, and Fire TV Cube. No root or special hardware required. Just enable unknown sources, use Downloader or the ES File Explorer APK manager, and you have access to any IPTV player.
