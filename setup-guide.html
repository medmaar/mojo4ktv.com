Step-by-Step Setup by Device

Step 1: Download IPTV Smarters app from the App Store:
Smarters Player Lite on App Store
Or search the App Store for: Smarters Player Lite

Step 2: Open the app once downloaded and select Login With Xtream Codes API.

Step 3: Enter the login details we provided after your subscription. Then click "ADD USER" to proceed.

Step 4: Wait a few seconds for the channels to load.

Step 5: Click the "Live TV" icon to start watching.

Step 6: Select the channel group you want to watch.

Step 7: Click on the channel name, then double-tap the small screen to go full screen.

Step 8: No need to add any EPG URL — just click "Install EPG".

For Samsung Smart TV:

  1. Press the Smart Hub button on your remote.
  2. Search for IPTV Smarters Pro and install it.
  3. Choose Login With Xtream Codes.
  4. Enter your Username, Password and Portal URL that we provide.

For LG Smart TV:

  1. Press the Home button and go to LG Content Store.
  2. Search for IPTV Smarters Pro and install it.
  3. Choose Login With Xtream Codes.
  4. Enter your Username, Password and Portal URL that we provide.

Step 3: Send us the MAC address via WhatsApp, Email, or Live Chat.

Step 4: After a few minutes, restart your TV and open the app. You're done!

Step 1: From the Fire TV home screen, hover over Find and click Search.

Step 3: Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install Unknown Apps and enable Downloader.

https://www.iptvsmarters.com/smarters.apk

Step 5: Wait for the file to download, then click Install and Done.

Step 1: When the box loads, click Settings and press the SETUP/SET button on your remote.

Step 2: Click System Settings → Servers → Portals.

Step 3: In the Portal 1 Name field enter a name. In Portal 1 URL enter the portal URL we provide.

Step 4: To activate, send us the MAC address from the label on the back of your box. We'll activate it remotely and send you the portal URL.

Step 5: Click OK to save, then press EXIT on your remote.

Step 6: Reboot your box and choose OK to apply changes.

Using KODI Krypton 17:

  1. Launch the Kodi app.
  2. In the left menu, click TV.
  3. Click Add-on browser.
  4. Click PVR IPTV Simple Client.
  5. Click Configure.
  6. Select General.
  7. Enter the IPTV subscription URL we provide.
  8. Wait for the channels to load.
