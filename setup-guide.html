Step-by-Step Setup by Device

Step 1: Download IPTV Smarters app from the App Store:
Smarters Player Lite on App Store
Or search the App Store for: Smarters Player Lite

Step 2: Open the app once downloaded and select Login With Xtream Codes API.

Step 3: Enter the login details we provided after your subscription. Then click "ADD USER" to proceed.

Step 4: Wait a few seconds for the channels to load.

Step 5: Click the "Live TV" icon to start watching.

Step 6: Select the channel group you want to watch.

Step 7: Click on the channel name, then double-tap the small screen to go full screen.

Step 8: No need to add any EPG URL — just click "Install EPG".
