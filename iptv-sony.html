How to Set Up IPTV on Sony Bravia TV

1

Identify your Sony Bravia OS

Modern Sony Bravia (2021+) runs Google TV. Older models (2015-2020) run Android TV. Both support IPTV from the Play Store.

2

Open Google Play Store

On your Sony Bravia, open Google Play Store. Search 'TiviMate' for Google TV / Android TV models — it has the best Sony Bravia interface.

3

Install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro

Download TiviMate (free, with optional Premium upgrade) or IPTV Smarters Pro (free). Both apps display beautifully on Sony Bravia 4K and 8K screens.

4

Add Mojo 4K via Xtream Codes

In TiviMate: Settings → Playlists → Add → Xtream Codes. Enter your server URL, username and password. In Smarters: Add User → Xtream Codes API.

5

Stream in 4K or 8K on Sony Bravia

Sony Bravia XR 8K models upscale IPTV content via Cognitive Processor XR. Browse your channels and enjoy sports, movies and TV in outstanding Sony quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IPTV work on Sony Bravia TV?
Yes — Sony Bravia Google TV and Android TV models (2015+) support IPTV via Google Play apps. TiviMate is the best app for Sony Bravia — it uses Android TV's interface perfectly. Install from Play Store and enter your Mojo 4K credentials.
What is the best IPTV app for Sony Bravia?
TiviMate is the best IPTV app for Sony Bravia Google TV and Android TV — it has a lean-back TV-optimized interface with full EPG guide, 4K playback, and works perfectly with the Sony remote. IPTV Smarters Pro is the best free alternative.
How do I install IPTV on Sony Bravia without Android TV?
Older Sony Bravia Smart TVs without Android TV can use Smart IPTV app (installed via USB), or connect a Firestick 4K / Apple TV 4K via HDMI for full IPTV access.
Does Sony Bravia support 4K IPTV?
Yes — Sony Bravia 4K and 8K models fully support 4K IPTV streams. TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro pass through 4K streams natively on compatible Sony Bravia models with HDMI 2.1 and 4K60Hz support.
