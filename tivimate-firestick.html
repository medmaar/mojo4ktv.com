How to Install TiviMate on Firestick

TiviMate is available directly in the Amazon App Store on all Firestick models — no sideloading required. From your Firestick home screen, open the App Store, search 'TiviMate', and install the free version in under 2 minutes.

TiviMate is widely considered the best IPTV app for Firestick. Its interface is perfectly sized for TV screens, the side EPG panel shows what's on next, multi-view lets you watch multiple channels simultaneously, and TiviMate Premium (~$4.99/year) adds catch-up TV and DVR recording.
