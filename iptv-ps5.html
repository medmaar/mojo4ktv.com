IPTV on PS5 & Xbox: What Works in 2026

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Short answer: Xbox can run IPTV directly, PS5 cannot. The PlayStation Store has no M3U or Xtream Codes player, so on a PS5 you need a workaround or a cheap streaming stick in another HDMI port. On Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, apps from the Microsoft Store such as Kodi and MyIPTV Player load an IPTV playlist. Here is how each option works.

Can You Watch IPTV on PS5?

Not natively. At the time of writing the PlayStation Store does not list an IPTV playlist player, and the PS5 has no general-purpose web browser you can rely on. The options that do work:

  • Add a streaming stick to a free HDMI port on the same TV. A Firestick with TiviMate is the most reliable route and switching inputs takes one button.
  • Use the TV itself. Most Samsung, LG and Android TVs can install an IPTV app directly; see IPTV for Smart TV.
  • Media server apps. Plex and similar apps are on PS5, but they need your own server set up on a computer to relay live TV, which is more work than most people want (see Plex IPTV).

Be wary of videos promising a "PS5 IPTV app": they usually rely on hidden browser tricks that break with firmware updates.

IPTV on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on the Xbox and install MyIPTV Player or Kodi.
  2. MyIPTV Player: go to Settings, add a new remote channel list, paste your M3U link and the EPG link, then select it.
  3. Kodi: enable the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on and paste your M3U link into its settings (full steps in Kodi IPTV).
  4. Restart the app and open the channel list.
