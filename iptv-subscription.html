How to Choose the Right IPTV Subscription

The right IPTV subscription depends on four variables: how long you want to commit (monthly vs annual), how many devices you need to stream simultaneously, what content matters most to you, and what your budget is. A 1-month 1-device plan at $9 is ideal for trying the service. A 12-month 3-device plan at $105/year ($8.75/month per household) is the most popular choice for families replacing cable.

The single most important decision is duration. Monthly plans offer flexibility; annual plans save 45–55% compared to rolling monthly. For most subscribers who test the service first with a free 24-hour trial, the annual plan is the obvious choice.

IPTV Subscription Plans Explained

Mojo 4K offers four subscription durations — 1 month ($9), 3 months ($22), 6 months ($34), and 12 months ($49) — each available with 1 to 5 simultaneous connections. The number of connections (devices) determines how many screens in your household can stream at the same time, not how many devices you can install the app on. You can install the app on unlimited devices; only the active streams count toward your limit.
