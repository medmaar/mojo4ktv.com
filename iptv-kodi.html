IPTV in Every Media Centre

Kodi IPTV (PVR IPTV Simple)

Install the PVR IPTV Simple Client addon in Kodi. Go to Settings → Live TV → PVR IPTV Simple Client → Configure, enter your Mojo 4K M3U URL. All 50,000+ channels appear in Kodi's live TV section with full EPG.

Plex IPTV

Plex supports live TV via its Live TV & DVR feature with an M3U IPTV source. Add your Mojo 4K M3U URL as a custom network tuner in Plex Media Server settings to watch all channels through the Plex interface.

Stremio IPTV

Stremio has IPTV addons (External Players + IPTV addon) that accept M3U URLs. Install the IPTV addon in Stremio and add your Mojo 4K M3U playlist to access all live channels.

Jellyfin IPTV

Jellyfin supports live TV via an M3U IPTV tuner. Add your Mojo 4K M3U URL as a live TV source in Jellyfin's Dashboard → Live TV → Tuner Devices. Full channel list and EPG guide included.
