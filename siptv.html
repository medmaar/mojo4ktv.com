SIPTV IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SIPTV?
SIPTV (Smart IPTV) is a dedicated IPTV player app for Samsung Tizen and LG webOS Smart TVs. It reads M3U playlists and displays channels with an EPG guide. Also known as my.siptv.eu.
What is SS IPTV?
SS IPTV is another popular Smart TV IPTV player available on Samsung, LG, Android TV and Apple TV. It supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes, making it compatible with Mojo 4K.
What is Set IPTV?
Set IPTV is a simple M3U player app available on Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen). It's one of the easiest ways to load an M3U playlist onto a Samsung TV without a sideloaded APK.
Does Mojo 4K work with SIPTV?
Yes — Mojo 4K provides an M3U playlist URL that works directly with SIPTV, SS IPTV and Set IPTV. Enter your M3U URL in the app's playlist settings and your channels load instantly.
Is SIPTV free?
SIPTV has a one-time activation fee of approximately €5.49 for the Samsung/LG app. SS IPTV and Set IPTV are free. All of them require a separate IPTV subscription like Mojo 4K.
