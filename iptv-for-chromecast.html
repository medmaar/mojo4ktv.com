IPTV on Chromecast with Google TV

The Chromecast with Google TV (4K or HD model) runs a full Android TV-based operating system — meaning you can install IPTV apps directly from the Google Play Store, just like on any Android TV device. This makes it one of the easiest smart streaming devices to set up for IPTV.

Method 1: IPTV Smarters Pro on Chromecast Google TV

  1. On your Chromecast with Google TV, open the Google Play Store
  2. Search for "IPTV Smarters Pro" and install the free app
  3. Open the app and select "Login via Xtream Codes API"
  4. Enter your Mojo 4K server URL, username and password
  5. Tap "Add User" — channels load in 30–60 seconds

Method 2: Cast IPTV from iPhone or Android

For older Chromecast models (1st, 2nd, 3rd generation) without Google TV, the easiest approach is casting from your phone. Open IPTV Smarters Pro on your Android or iPhone with your Mojo 4K subscription loaded. Tap the Cast icon and select your Chromecast. The stream mirrors to your TV through your phone's connection.

Best IPTV App for Chromecast with Google TV

IPTV Smarters Pro and XCIPTV are both excellent on Chromecast with Google TV. XCIPTV has been specifically optimized for Google TV's interface and navigation style, making it feel more native on Chromecast. TiviMate, the preferred choice for Firestick users, also works on Chromecast with Google TV via the Google Play Store.

Chromecast vs Firestick for IPTV

The Chromecast with Google TV 4K and Firestick 4K Max are similarly priced and both excellent for IPTV. The key difference: Chromecast uses the Google TV interface (cleaner, more Google-integrated) while Firestick uses Amazon's Fire OS. Both support TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro. If you're already in the Amazon ecosystem, Firestick may feel more natural; if you prefer Google's interface, Chromecast is an excellent choice.

How to Set Up Mojo 4K on Chromecast and Google TV

  1. 1
    Identify your Chromecast model — Chromecast with Google TV (2020+) runs a full Android TV OS — install apps directly. Original Chromecast 1/2/3 requires casting from a phone or tablet. This guide covers Chromecast with Google TV.
  2. 2
    Open Google Play Store on your Chromecast with Google TV — From your Chromecast with Google TV home screen, go to the app search area and open Google Play Store.
  3. 3
    Install IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate — Search for and install IPTV Smarters Pro (free) or TiviMate (free with optional Premium). Both work natively on Chromecast with Google TV.
  4. 4
    Enter Mojo 4K credentials via Xtream Codes — Open your IPTV app, select Add Playlist or Xtream Codes, and enter your Mojo 4K server URL, username and password received via WhatsApp.
  5. 5
    For original Chromecast: cast from phone — Install IPTV Smarters Pro on your Android or iOS phone. Enter your Mojo 4K credentials. During playback, tap the Cast icon to send the stream to your TV via the original Chromecast.

**Best pick:** The Chromecast with Google TV 4K ($49) runs full Android TV — TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro install directly like on a Firestick. It is the recommended Chromecast model for IPTV.
