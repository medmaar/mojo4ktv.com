What's in an IPTV Account?

An IPTV account (also called an IPTV subscription or IPTV line) consists of login credentials that give your IPTV player app access to streams. Specifically, a Mojo 4K IPTV account includes:

Xtream Codes credentials: a server URL, username, and password — entered once into TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, or any compatible app. M3U playlist URL: a direct link to your channel list, compatible with VLC, Kodi, Smart IPTV, and any M3U player.

Your IPTV account credentials are delivered to your WhatsApp or email within 60 seconds of payment. Keep them safe — they are your permanent login for the duration of your subscription. If you lose them, contact our WhatsApp support for instant re-delivery.
