How IP Television Works

Traditional television broadcasts all channels simultaneously over a fixed infrastructure — cable, satellite, or over-the-air. Every household receives the same signal and switches between channels locally. This works but is expensive to build, maintain, and upgrade.

IP television works differently. Instead of broadcasting all channels simultaneously, it sends only the channel you are watching as a data stream over your existing broadband connection. When you switch channels, a new stream starts. The video is encoded as IP packets, transmitted over the internet, received by your device, decoded, and displayed — all in real time with a typical delay of 2-5 seconds.

The result: any content that can be encoded as data can be delivered via IP television. No geographic limits, no cable infrastructure required, unlimited channel capacity. A single IPTV provider can deliver 50,000 channels globally to any device with an internet connection.

IP Television — Frequently Asked Questions

What is IP television?

IP television (IPTV) is the delivery of television content over internet protocol networks. Instead of cable or satellite infrastructure, your TV channels arrive through your broadband connection as data streams. Mojo 4K delivers 50,000+ live channels via IP television from $9/month.

How does IP television differ from cable TV?

Cable TV broadcasts all channels simultaneously over a fixed wire to your home. IP television sends only the channel you are watching as a stream over your existing internet connection. IP television offers more channels (50,000+ vs 500), lower cost ($9 vs $70-100/month), international content, and works on any device worldwide.

Do I need a special device for IP television?

No special device is required. IP television works on Amazon Firestick, Samsung Smart TV, LG Smart TV, Apple TV, iPhone, Android, Chromecast, Roku, and PC. Download a compatible app (TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro), enter your credentials, and stream immediately.

Is IP television legal?

Licensed IP television services are legal. Mojo 4K operates as a licensed IPTV provider. The technology itself — IP television — is completely legal and used by major providers including YouTube TV, Netflix, Hulu, and Sling TV. The legality depends on whether the content is properly licensed, not on the delivery method.
