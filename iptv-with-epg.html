What is an EPG in IPTV?

EPG stands for Electronic Programme Guide. It's the on-screen TV guide that shows programme schedules for every channel — what's on now, what's coming next and a full 7-day lookahead. Mojo 4K includes EPG data for all 50,000+ channels, updated every few hours automatically.

How to Enable EPG on Your IPTV App

TiviMate: go to Settings → EPG → Add EPG URL (use the EPG link sent with your credentials). IPTV Smarters Pro: the EPG loads automatically from your Xtream Codes playlist. Smart IPTV: EPG loads during channel sync. GSE Smart IPTV: add the XMLTV EPG URL in settings. All Mojo 4K plans include EPG access.
