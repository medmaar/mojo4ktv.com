MyIPTV4K IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

What is MyIPTV4K?
MyIPTV4K is a paid IPTV player app available on Windows (Microsoft Store), Android TV, and Samsung Smart TVs. It supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes API, and is popular for its clean interface and 4K playback.
Is MyIPTV4K free?
MyIPTV4K has a one-time purchase fee (approximately £2.49 on Windows). The IPTV channels are provided separately via a subscription like Mojo 4K, starting from $9/month.
Does MyIPTV4K work with Mojo 4K?
Yes — Mojo 4K provides both Xtream Codes credentials and M3U playlist URLs. Both connection methods work with MyIPTV4K across Windows and Android versions.
Can I use MyIPTV4K on Windows?
Yes — MyIPTV4K is available on the Microsoft Store for Windows 10 and Windows 11. It's a popular choice for IPTV on PC alongside TiviMate (via Android emulator) and VLC.
