What Is an M3U URL?

An M3U URL (also called an M3U playlist link or M3U link) is a web address that, when opened in an IPTV player, loads a complete list of live TV channel streams. The format was originally developed for audio playlists (the "MP3 URL" format) but has been adapted as the universal standard for IPTV channel distribution. Every Mojo 4K subscription includes a unique M3U URL alongside Xtream Codes credentials.

What Your Mojo 4K M3U URL Looks Like

Your personal M3U URL follows this structure:
http://server.mojo4ktv.com:PORT/get.php?username=YOUR_USER&password=YOUR_PASS&type=m3u_plus&output=ts
This URL is sent to you via WhatsApp within 60 seconds of purchase and includes your personal login credentials embedded in the link.

How to Use Your IPTV M3U URL — Every App

TiviMate (Firestick / Android TV)

Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist → M3U URL. Paste your Mojo 4K M3U URL. For better EPG accuracy, use Xtream Codes instead of M3U in TiviMate — it auto-loads all programme data.

VLC Media Player (PC / Mac / Mobile)

Media → Open Network Stream → paste M3U URL → Play. VLC works as a basic M3U URL player on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android. No EPG, but zero setup cost for quick channel access.

Smart IPTV (Samsung & LG TV)

Open Smart IPTV app on your TV → go to siptv.eu on your browser → enter your TV's MAC address and paste your M3U URL → click Send List. All channels load with logos and categories.

IPTV Smarters Pro (All Devices)

Add User → Load Your Playlist / Xtream Codes → M3U URL. Paste your M3U URL and tap Add User. Works on Android, iOS, Windows and Mac with full M3U_plus category and logo support.

Kodi PVR (IPTV Simple Client)

Add-ons → PVR IPTV Simple Client → Configure → Location: Remote Path → paste M3U URL. Set EPG URL separately. Kodi then shows all Mojo 4K channels in the TV section.

GSE Smart IPTV (iOS & Android)

Remote Playlists → + → Add M3U URL. Paste your M3U URL and name the playlist. Full M3U_plus support for channel logos, categories and EPG data from Mojo 4K.

M3U URL Format — Explained

Your Mojo 4K M3U URL follows this standard format:

# Standard M3U URL
http://[server]:8080/get.php?username=[USER]&password=[PASS]&type=m3u
# M3U_plus format (with channel logos + categories)
http://[server]:8080/get.php?username=[USER]&password=[PASS]&type=m3u_plus
# Always use m3u_plus — it includes tvg-logo, tvg-name, group-title metadata

type=m3u

Basic channel list. No logos, no categories, no EPG IDs. Works in all M3U players.

type=m3u_plus ✅

Extended format with tvg-logo, tvg-id (for EPG), group-title (categories). Use this always.

Open VLC on your PC or Mac, go to Media → Open Network Stream (Ctrl+N), paste your Mojo 4K M3U URL and click Play. All channels load as a playlist in the sidebar. For better channel organization, use the Playlist view under View → Playlist.

Your M3U URL works on any number of devices for playback — however, simultaneous streams are limited by your plan (1 to 5 devices). You can install the app on unlimited devices; the limit applies to how many streams run at the same time.

Your Mojo 4K M3U URL does not change unless you request a reset. The URL is permanently linked to your account credentials. When you renew your subscription, the same URL continues to work with no changes needed.

M3U and M3U8 are the same format — M3U8 specifies UTF-8 encoding, while M3U can use various encodings. Most modern IPTV players accept both. Your Mojo 4K URL can be used as either by changing the extension in apps that require it; the content is identical.
