How to Add Mojo 4K IPTV to Kodi — Step by Step

  1. 1
    Subscribe to Mojo 4K and get your M3U URLVisit /pricing or start a free 24-hour trial. Your M3U_plus URL and EPG URL arrive via WhatsApp in 60 seconds. Format: http://server/get.php?username=USER&password=PASS&type=m3u_plus
  2. 2
    Open Kodi → Add-ons → DownloadIn Kodi, select the Add-ons icon (box icon) from the left sidebar, then choose Download from repository (the open box icon).
  3. 3
    Install PVR IPTV Simple ClientNavigate to PVR Clients → PVR IPTV Simple Client → Install. Wait for installation to complete and click OK when prompted to configure.
  4. 4
    Configure with your Mojo 4K M3U URLIn settings: General tab → M3U playlist URL → select Remote Path → paste your M3U_plus URL. EPG settings tab → XMLTV URL → paste your Mojo 4K EPG URL (format: http://server/xmltv.php?username=USER&password=PASS). Click OK.
  5. 5
    Restart KodiRestart Kodi completely (close and reopen). The PVR client will download your channel list and EPG data.
  6. 6
    Browse Mojo 4K channels in Kodi TVSelect TV from Kodi's main menu. All 50,000+ Mojo 4K channels appear with EPG programme guide. Browse by category or use the EPG grid to find what you want to watch.

Kodi IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add IPTV to Kodi?

Install PVR IPTV Simple Client from the official Kodi repository. Configure it with your Mojo 4K M3U_plus URL (General tab) and EPG XMLTV URL (EPG tab). Restart Kodi and all 50,000+ channels appear in the TV section with programme guide.

Does Mojo 4K work with Kodi?

Yes. Mojo 4K provides an M3U_plus URL that works with Kodi's PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on. All 50,000+ channels load in Kodi's Live TV section with full EPG data from the Mojo 4K XMLTV feed.

What is the best IPTV add-on for Kodi?

PVR IPTV Simple Client is the official and most reliable IPTV add-on for Kodi, available directly from the official Kodi repository. It accepts M3U and XMLTV EPG URLs, making it compatible with Mojo 4K out of the box.

Do I need a VPN for IPTV on Kodi?

No VPN is required to use Mojo 4K on Kodi. Mojo 4K is a licensed IPTV service — you stream it directly without additional tools. A VPN can sometimes cause buffering by adding latency. Only consider a VPN if your ISP specifically throttles IPTV traffic on your connection.
