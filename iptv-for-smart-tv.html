IPTV on Smart TVs — What You Need to Know

Modern smart TVs run different operating systems (Tizen on Samsung, webOS on LG, VIDAA on Hisense, Android TV on Sony/Philips), and IPTV app availability varies by platform. Here's the setup approach for each major smart TV brand with Mojo 4K.

Samsung Smart TV (Tizen OS)

Samsung Tizen TVs (2016 and newer) support IPTV via Smart IPTV (SIPTV) and SS IPTV, both available in the Samsung App Store. Download either app, enter your Mojo 4K M3U URL, and activate via the SS IPTV or Smart IPTV website. Channels load in under 2 minutes. For newer Samsung TVs (2022+), the Samsung Internet browser can also load web-based IPTV players directly.

LG Smart TV (webOS)

LG webOS TVs (2016–2026) support IPTV through Smart IPTV and SS IPTV from the LG Content Store. The setup is identical to Samsung: download the app, paste your M3U URL, and activate. LG's webOS 6.0 and newer (2022+ models) also support casting from IPTV Smarters Pro on an iPhone or Android phone directly to the TV via LG's built-in casting feature.

Sony, Philips & Hisense (Android TV / VIDAA)

Android TV devices (Sony Bravia, Philips Ambilight Android TVs, select TCL models) can install IPTV Smarters Pro, XCIPTV, or TiviMate directly from the Google Play Store — making setup identical to an Android phone. Hisense VIDAA TVs have a more limited app ecosystem but support the SS IPTV app for M3U playback.

Best Smart TV for IPTV in 2026

If you're buying a new TV specifically for IPTV, Android TV models (Sony, Philips, or any TV with Google TV) give the best IPTV experience because they run TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro natively with full EPG and catch-up support. Samsung and LG TVs require workaround apps (SS IPTV, Smart IPTV) that are less feature-rich than TiviMate. The simplest solution for any non-Android TV is a £40 Amazon Firestick 4K plugged into an HDMI port.
