Best IPTV for Apple TV 4K 2026 — Complete Setup Guide

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Best IPTV for Apple TV 4K in 2026. Stream 50,000+ channels on tvOS using Infuse, IPTV Smarters, or the web portal. AirPlay from iPhone also works. Free 24h trial.

IPTV Apps Available on Apple TV 4K

Apple TV 4K runs tvOS, which has a more restrictive app ecosystem than Android TV. However, several excellent IPTV players are available on the tvOS App Store. Infuse 7 (from Firecore) is the most polished option — it supports M3U playlists natively and delivers beautiful 4K HDR playback. IPTV Smarters Pro has a tvOS version available on the App Store that supports full Xtream Codes API login. GSE Smart IPTV also has an Apple TV version.

Setting Up Infuse with Mojo 4K on Apple TV

  1. Download Infuse 7 from the tvOS App Store (free with in-app purchase for Pro)
  2. Open Infuse and go to Settings → Add Source
  3. Select "HTTP/HTTPS" and paste your Mojo 4K M3U URL
  4. Infuse loads all channels as a media library — browse Live TV by category
  5. Infuse Pro ($9.99/year) adds full EPG guide integration

Setting Up IPTV Smarters Pro on Apple TV

  1. Search "IPTV Smarters Pro" in the Apple TV App Store and install
  2. Open the app and tap "Login via Xtream Codes API"
  3. Enter your Mojo 4K server URL, username, and password
  4. Channels and EPG load automatically within 60 seconds

AirPlay IPTV to Apple TV from iPhone

An alternative to native Apple TV apps is AirPlay mirroring. Open IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV on your iPhone with your Mojo 4K subscription loaded. Tap the AirPlay icon in the player and select your Apple TV. The stream plays on your TV in full resolution. This works on any Apple TV model (2nd generation and newer) even without native IPTV app support.

Apple TV 4K vs Firestick for IPTV

Apple TV 4K has the best hardware of any IPTV streaming device — the A15 Bionic chip delivers significantly faster UI performance than the Firestick 4K Max's MediaTek chip. However, the IPTV app ecosystem on tvOS is less mature than Firestick's Android-based apps. TiviMate (the preferred IPTV player for most users) is not available on tvOS. For Apple TV users who also have an iPhone, the AirPlay approach combined with IPTV Smarters on iPhone is an effective hybrid solution.

How to Set Up Mojo 4K on Apple TV

  1. 1
    Open the App Store on Apple TV — From the Apple TV home screen, navigate to the App Store. Ensure your Apple TV is running tvOS 14 or later for best app compatibility.
  2. 2
    Install IPTV Smarters Pro — Search for 'IPTV Smarters Pro' in the App Store. Install the free app — it is compatible with Apple TV 4, Apple TV 4K (all generations) and runs natively on tvOS.
  3. 3
    Open IPTV Smarters Pro and select Add User — Launch IPTV Smarters Pro on your Apple TV. Select Add New User, then choose Xtream Codes API as the playlist type.
  4. 4
    Enter your Mojo 4K credentials — Input your server URL, username and password from the WhatsApp message you received after subscribing. Tap Add User to confirm.
  5. 5
    Browse all 50,000+ channels — Your full Mojo 4K channel library loads with EPG data. Navigate sports, entertainment and international channels with the Apple TV Siri Remote. 4K HDR streams play natively on Apple TV 4K.

**Alternative app:** IPTVX is another tvOS IPTV app available on the App Store. It supports Xtream Codes and M3U playlists and has a clean Apple TV interface optimised for the Siri Remote.
