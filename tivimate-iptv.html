TiviMate Advanced Features — DVR, Multi-View & M3U

TiviMate Premium unlocks several features that standard IPTV players lack. The built-in DVR lets you schedule recordings directly from the EPG — tap any future programme in the guide and record it to local storage on your Firestick or Android device. Multi-view puts up to 4 channels on screen simultaneously, perfect for watching multiple sports at once. TiviMate also accepts both M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes API credentials, giving you full flexibility in how you load your Mojo 4K subscription. The M3U URL for your subscription is available in your account dashboard alongside the Xtream Codes server, username and password.

Catch-up TV is another standout feature when your IPTV provider supports it. With Mojo 4K and TiviMate, you can rewind live TV and access the last 24–72 hours of content on major sports and news channels — no separate recording needed. TiviMate's companion app (available on Google Play) lets you browse your guide and schedule recordings from your phone while streaming on the TV.
