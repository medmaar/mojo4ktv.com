How to Set Up IPTV on MAG 254 / MAG 256

1

Find your MAG 254 MAC address

Look at the sticker on the bottom of your MAG 254/256. The MAC address is in the format 00:1A:79:XX:XX:XX. Note it down — you need it for portal activation.

2

Contact Mojo 4K with your MAC

Send your MAC address to Mojo 4K via WhatsApp. Your MAG portal line is activated within minutes.

3

Open MAG settings

On your MAG remote, go to MENU → Settings → System Settings → Servers → Stalker Portal.

4

Enter portal URL

Clear the existing URL and type your Mojo 4K portal URL. Press OK to save.

5

Reboot and access portal

Restart your MAG 254/256. The portal loads with all channels, EPG, and VOD organized by category.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up IPTV on MAG 254?
1) Find your MAC address on the bottom label. 2) Subscribe to Mojo 4K and provide your MAC address via WhatsApp. 3) Go to Settings → Servers → Stalker Portal on your MAG 254. 4) Enter the portal URL provided. 5) Reboot and enjoy all 50,000+ channels.
What is the max resolution on MAG 254?
The MAG 254 supports up to 1080p Full HD. For 4K Ultra HD IPTV, upgrade to MAG 420, 424 or 425 which natively support 4K streams.
Can I use Mojo 4K on MAG 254 without the portal?
Yes — via STBemu app on Android (which emulates MAG 254) or via IPTV Smarters Pro using your M3U URL. However, using the native Stalker Portal on your MAG 254 gives the best performance.
Does MAG 254 support catch-up TV?
Yes — Mojo 4K's portal supports catch-up TV and time-shift on MAG 254/256 where channels have catch-up enabled. This lets you rewind or watch content from the last 7 days.
