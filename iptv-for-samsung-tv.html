Setup Guide

How to Set Up Mojo 4K on Samsung Smart TV

  1. 1
    Open the Samsung App Store (Smart Hub) — Press the Home button on your Samsung remote. Navigate to the Apps section to open the Samsung App Store (Smart Hub).
  2. 2
    Search for Smart IPTV or SS IPTV — In the App Store search, type 'Smart IPTV' or 'SS IPTV'. Both apps accept M3U URLs and work with Mojo 4K. Install your preferred app.
  3. 3
    Get your Mojo 4K M3U URL — Your M3U playlist URL is included with your subscription and was sent via WhatsApp. Format: http://server/get.php?username=USER&password=PASS&type=m3u_plus
  4. 4
    Register your M3U on the Smart IPTV portal — On a browser, visit siptv.eu. Enter your Samsung TV's MAC address (shown in the Smart IPTV app) and paste your Mojo 4K M3U URL. Click Send List.
  5. 5
    Restart the app and browse channels — Relaunch Smart IPTV on your Samsung TV. All 50,000+ Mojo 4K channels load with logos and categories. A one-time Smart IPTV activation fee (~$5.49) applies.

**Alternative method:** For newer Samsung Tizen TVs (2017+), install IPTV Smarters Pro from the Samsung App Store. It supports Xtream Codes — the faster setup that doesn't require the Smart IPTV portal.
