Formuler Z8, Z8 Pro, Z10 SE & Older Models: IPTV Setup

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Formuler boxes are built for IPTV, and older models such as the Z8, Z8 Pro, Z10 SE and Z7+ still work well with a modern subscription through MyTVOnline. The setup is the same on every model. What differs is speed, app support and whether an upgrade to a Z11 is worth it, which this guide covers model by model.

IPTV Setup on Formuler Z8 and Z10 SE (MyTVOnline)

  1. Open MyTVOnline (MyTVOnline 2 on older models, MyTVOnline 3 on newer firmware).
  2. Choose Add Portal / Add Playlist, then Xtream Codes (or "XC").
  3. Enter a nickname, the server URL, username and password.
  4. Save, select the new portal and wait for channels and guide data to load.
  5. Optional: in MyTVOnline settings, enable the EPG preload so the guide is ready each morning.

Mojo 4K sends Xtream Codes credentials, an M3U link and, for MAG-type devices, a portal URL by WhatsApp or email within minutes. Try it first with the free 24-hour trial.

MyTVOnline 2 vs MyTVOnline 3

MyTVOnline 3 brings a refreshed interface and better catch-up handling, and ships on newer Formuler models. Many older boxes stay on MyTVOnline 2, which still supports Xtream Codes and M3U perfectly well. If your model cannot update, nothing about your subscription changes. More on the app itself in MyTVOnline.

Running TiviMate or Smarters on a Formuler

Formuler boxes run Android, so you can also install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters from the store. On a Z8 or Z7+ these heavier apps can feel slow; MyTVOnline is usually smoother on older hardware.

When to Upgrade from a Z8

  • Menus take several seconds to respond or apps crash after updates.
  • You bought a 4K HDR TV and want smooth 4K sport at 50 or 60 fps.
  • Wi-Fi drops often and you cannot run Ethernet to the box.

If two of those apply, a Z11 Pro or a Firestick 4K Max is a sensible upgrade. Our best IPTV box ranking compares options.
