SET IPTV Setup Guide: Samsung, LG & Android TV

Last updated: November 6, 2026

SET IPTV is a Smart TV player for Samsung, LG and Android TV. It contains no channels: you link it to your own playlist by entering the TV's MAC address and your M3U link on the developer's website. Setup takes about five minutes and the steps below work for any provider that gives you an M3U link.

What SET IPTV Is (and Is Not)

SET IPTV is a playlist player, in the same family as SS IPTV and SIPTV and Smart IPTV. It is popular because it runs on TVs where TiviMate cannot be installed, and because the playlist is managed from a browser instead of typed with a TV remote.

  • Runs on: Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS) and Android TV, depending on what your TV's app store lists.
  • Loads: M3U playlists and an XMLTV programme guide.
  • Cost: the developer usually offers a trial period and then a one-time activation fee. Check the price shown on the developer's site, as it is set by them, not by your IPTV provider.

How to Set Up SET IPTV in 5 Steps

  1. Install SET IPTV from the Samsung Apps store, LG Content Store or Google Play on your TV.
  2. Open the app and note the MAC address shown on the first screen.
  3. On your phone or computer, open the playlist upload page on the developer's website (the address is shown inside the app).
  4. Enter the MAC address, paste your M3U link and, if offered, your EPG (XMLTV) link, then submit.
  5. Restart SET IPTV on the TV. Channels and the guide load within a minute.

SET IPTV vs Other Smart TV Apps

If SET IPTV is not listed in your TV's store, or you prefer typing credentials directly, these apps do the same job: IBO Player, Smart IPTV, SS IPTV and Smart STB (portal-based, closest to a MAG box). For the most features on a TV, a Firestick with TiviMate plugged into HDMI still wins.
