MAG 524 and Other 4K MAG Boxes: IPTV Setup

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The MAG 524 is Infomir's current 4K box and, with the MAG 520, 424 and 425A, the family most IPTV users should buy today. All of them log in with a portal URL, so setup takes two minutes once your provider has registered the box's MAC address. This guide explains the models, the "w1/w3" suffixes and the portal setup.

Portal Setup on MAG 524 (and 520, 424)

  1. Find the MAC address on the sticker under the box (format 00:1A:79:XX:XX:XX) and send it to your provider.
  2. On the box, open Settings > Servers > Portals.
  3. Enter a portal name and the portal URL you receive.
  4. Save, go back and choose Restart portal.
  5. The portal loads with Live TV, VOD and the guide.

What "w1" and "w3" Mean

The suffix marks built-in Wi-Fi. Models without it need an Ethernet cable or a compatible USB Wi-Fi adapter. "w3" models have dual-band Wi-Fi; use 5 GHz for 4K. Even so, Ethernet remains the most reliable choice for live sport.

MAG 425A: When Android Helps

The MAG 425A runs Android TV, so besides the MAG portal you can install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters with Xtream Codes. If you want MAG-style menus on a TV without a box, Smart STB does it on Samsung and LG.

Common 4K MAG Errors

  • "STB blocked" / "Your STB is not authorised": the MAC is not registered yet or was typed wrong. Recheck it with support.
  • "Loading portal" forever: check the URL, then reboot the box and router.
  • 4K stutters: use Ethernet or 5 GHz, and see buffering fixes.
