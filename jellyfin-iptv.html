What is Jellyfin IPTV?

Jellyfin is a free, open-source media server that supports live IPTV through its Live TV feature. Unlike Plex, which requires a paid Plex Pass for live TV functionality, Jellyfin's M3U IPTV support is completely free — making it ideal for budget-conscious IPTV viewers.

Every Mojo 4K subscription includes an M3U playlist URL that works directly with Jellyfin's Live TV tuner. Simply add your M3U URL in the Jellyfin Dashboard under Live TV settings, and all 50,000+ channels appear across all your Jellyfin client devices.

While Jellyfin IPTV requires a server setup (a PC, NAS, or even a Raspberry Pi), it offers a unified media experience — watch your IPTV channels alongside your personal movie and TV show library, on any screen in your home.
