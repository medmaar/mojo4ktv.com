How to Set Up IPTV on Amazon Firestick 4K

1

Get Mojo 4K subscription

Visit Mojo4KTV.com and choose a plan from $9/month. Your credentials arrive via WhatsApp in under 60 seconds.

2

Download TiviMate on Firestick 4K

Go to Amazon App Store on your Firestick 4K, search 'TiviMate' and install it. Or sideload the APK via Downloader app.

3

Add your Mojo 4K playlist

Open TiviMate → Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist → Xtream Codes. Enter your server URL, username and password.

4

Enable EPG guide

In TiviMate settings, enable the EPG. Your programme guide updates automatically showing current and upcoming shows for all 50,000+ channels.

5

Watch in 4K on your Firestick 4K

Navigate to Sports or Entertainment categories and enjoy buffer-free 4K Ultra HD streaming on your Firestick 4K.

IPTV Firestick 4K — Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Firestick 4K better for IPTV than the regular Firestick?
Yes — the Firestick 4K has a faster processor, 2GB RAM (vs 1GB), 4K Ultra HD output, and Dolby Vision support, making it significantly better for IPTV. It handles 4K streams without buffering that the regular Firestick might struggle with.
What is the best IPTV app for Firestick 4K?
TiviMate is the best IPTV app for Firestick 4K — it's designed for Android TV with a beautiful interface, EPG guide, recording (premium), and smooth 4K playback. IPTV Smarters Pro is the best free alternative.
Does Mojo 4K IPTV work on Firestick 4K?
Yes — Mojo 4K is fully compatible with Firestick 4K. Our Xtream Codes credentials work with TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, Smart IPTV and any other Firestick IPTV app.
Can I get IPTV on Firestick 4K without jailbreaking?
Yes — no jailbreaking needed. TiviMate and IPTV Smarters Pro are available on the Amazon App Store. Just install, enter your Mojo 4K credentials, and stream 50,000+ channels immediately.
