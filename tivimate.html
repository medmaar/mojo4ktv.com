What is TiviMate?

TiviMate is an Android IPTV player app developed by TiviMate Corporation. It runs on Android TV boxes, Amazon Firestick, Google TV, and any Android device supporting Google Play. TiviMate connects to any IPTV subscription that provides Xtream Codes credentials or an M3U playlist URL, giving you a clean, TV-remote-friendly interface to browse and watch live channels.

Available in two tiers — TiviMate (free) for basic streaming, and TiviMate Premium (~$4.99/year) which adds multi-panel viewing, a full EPG programme guide, catch-up TV, and DVR recording to external storage. The premium unlock is available directly inside the app.
