Frequently Asked Questions

What is IPTV Smarters Lite?
IPTV Smarters Lite is a trimmed-down version of IPTV Smarters Pro, available free on Android and iOS. It supports Xtream Codes and M3U playlists. The Pro version adds features like external player support and multi-screen.
Is IPTV Smarters Lite free?
Yes — Smarters Lite is completely free to download and use. You need an IPTV subscription (like Mojo 4K) to provide channels. Mojo 4K starts from $9/month with a free 24h trial.
What's the difference between Smarters Lite and Smarters Pro?
Smarters Pro has additional features like external player support (VLC/MX Player), multi-screen casting, advanced EPG options, and parental controls. Both support Xtream Codes and M3U. For most users, Lite works perfectly.
Does Mojo 4K work with IPTV Smarters Lite?
Yes — Mojo 4K provides Xtream Codes credentials that connect directly to IPTV Smarters Lite. Select 'Login with Xtream Codes API' and enter your server URL, username and password.
