IPTV on Raspberry Pi with Kodi

The Raspberry Pi 4 and Raspberry Pi 5 are excellent IPTV devices when running Kodi via LibreELEC. LibreELEC is a lightweight Linux distribution purpose-built for Kodi, turning any Raspberry Pi into a dedicated home media centre with full IPTV support.

Setup requires installing LibreELEC on a microSD card, booting the Pi, then installing the PVR IPTV Simple Client addon in Kodi. Add your Mojo 4K M3U URL in the addon settings, and all 50,000+ channels load with EPG data into Kodi's live TV section.

Raspberry Pi 4 handles HD and most Full HD IPTV streams well. Raspberry Pi 5 handles 4K, though hardware video decoding for H.265/HEVC streams requires proper LibreELEC configuration. For a simpler IPTV setup, a Firestick 4K Max is easier — but Pi gives you maximum control.

1

Flash LibreELEC

Download LibreELEC for Raspberry Pi and flash to a microSD card using Raspberry Pi Imager. Boot your Pi.

2

Install Kodi PVR Addon

In LibreELEC/Kodi: Add-ons > My Add-ons > PVR Clients > PVR IPTV Simple Client > Install.

3

Add Your M3U URL

Subscribe to Mojo 4K and receive your M3U URL. Enter it in PVR IPTV Simple Client settings.

4

Watch IPTV in Kodi

Navigate to TV > Channels in Kodi. All 50,000+ channels appear with full EPG guide data.
