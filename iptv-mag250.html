MAG 250 & MAG 256: IPTV Setup and When to Upgrade

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The MAG 250 is one of the most widely used IPTV boxes ever made, and many are still running. It can still play IPTV through a portal, but it is an HD-era box without H.265 (HEVC) decoding, so some modern streams will not play on it. Here is how to set it up, what to expect, and how to choose a replacement.

The practical effect: H.264 channels in SD and HD play fine, while channels delivered only in H.265 or 4K will not. That is a hardware limit, not a subscription problem.

Setting Up IPTV on a MAG 250

  1. Send the MAC address printed under the box to your provider.
  2. Press Setup on the remote (or Settings from the menu) and open Servers > Portals.
  3. Enter a name and the portal URL in Portal 1.
  4. Save with OK, exit and choose Restart portal.

Mojo 4K sends Xtream Codes credentials, an M3U link and, for MAG-type devices, a portal URL by WhatsApp or email within minutes. Try it first with the free 24-hour trial.

If the menu looks different, update the box firmware first from the system settings; very old firmware can fail to load modern portals.

Signs It Is Time to Replace a MAG 250

  • Popular channels show "channel not available" while they work on your phone.
  • The portal takes a minute or more to load.
  • You now have a 4K TV.

The natural upgrades are the MAG 524 or 520 if you like MAG's simplicity, or a Firestick 4K Max with TiviMate if you want a modern guide and apps.
