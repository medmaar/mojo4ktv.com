Smarters Firestick IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

Is IPTV Smarters Pro available on Firestick?
Yes — IPTV Smarters Pro is available on the Amazon App Store and can be installed directly on any Firestick. Search 'IPTV Smarters Pro' in the Firestick search bar.
Is IPTV Smarters Pro free on Firestick?
Yes — IPTV Smarters Pro is completely free to install and use on Firestick. You need a separate IPTV subscription like Mojo 4K (from $9/month) to access channels.
Which Firestick models support IPTV Smarters Pro?
IPTV Smarters Pro works on all Amazon Firestick models: Firestick Lite, regular Firestick, Firestick 4K, Firestick 4K Max, and Fire TV Cube. It also works on Fire TV Edition smart TVs.
How do I add Mojo 4K to IPTV Smarters Pro on Firestick?
Open Smarters Pro on your Firestick, tap 'Add User' > 'Login with Xtream Codes API'. Enter your Mojo 4K server URL, username and password. Your channels load within 30 seconds.
Setup Guide

How to Install IPTV Smarters Pro on Firestick

  1. 1
    Enable apps from unknown sources on Firestick — Go to Firestick Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Apps from Unknown Sources → turn ON. This is required because IPTV Smarters Pro is not in the Amazon App Store on all regions.
  2. 2
    Install the Downloader app — In the Amazon App Store, search for 'Downloader' by AFTVnews. Install the free app — it allows you to sideload APK files on Firestick.
  3. 3
    Download IPTV Smarters Pro APK — Open Downloader, type the IPTV Smarters Pro download URL in the address bar and tap Go. The APK downloads and installs automatically. Tap Done, then Open to launch.
  4. 4
    Add your Mojo 4K credentials — In IPTV Smarters Pro, select Add User → Xtream Codes API. Enter your Mojo 4K server URL, username and password. Tap Add User. All 50,000+ channels load with EPG.
  5. 5
    Pin Smarters to your Firestick home screen — After first use, press and hold the IPTV Smarters Pro app icon. Select Add to Home. The app now appears on your Firestick home screen for quick access.

TiviMate is generally preferred over IPTV Smarters Pro on Firestick — it is available directly in the Amazon App Store, requires no sideloading, and has a cleaner TV interface. If you prefer IPTV Smarters Pro, follow the sideload steps above.
