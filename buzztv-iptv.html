BuzzTV XRS 4500 & XRS 4900: IPTV Setup

Last updated: November 6, 2026

BuzzTV boxes such as the XRS 4500 and XRS 4900 are Android-based IPTV boxes, popular in Canada and the US, that ship with BuzzTV's own IPTV player and a remote with dedicated live TV keys. You sign in with Xtream Codes or an M3U link like any other player. Here is the setup, what to tweak and what to do if the built-in player is not for you.

Adding Your Subscription to the BuzzTV Player

  1. Open the built-in BuzzTV IPTV app from the home screen.
  2. Go to Settings > Accounts (or Add Provider) and choose Xtream Codes.
  3. Enter a nickname, the server URL, username and password.
  4. Save and let channels, VOD and guide data load. The first guide download can take a few minutes.

Mojo 4K sends Xtream Codes credentials, an M3U link and, for MAG-type devices, a portal URL by WhatsApp or email within minutes. Try it first with the free 24-hour trial.

Settings and Remote Tips

  • Guide colours and time shift: set the EPG offset if listings look an hour off for your province or state.
  • Favourites: build a favourites group for each person in the house so the channel list stays short.
  • Player engine: if a channel stutters, switch the decoder setting and retry.
  • Ethernet: wired connections are noticeably more stable for 4K sport.

Using TiviMate or Smarters on BuzzTV

Because the boxes run Android, you can also install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters and use the same login. Some users keep the BuzzTV player for its remote shortcuts and use TiviMate for recordings.
