IPTV Legal Status — Frequently Asked Questions

Is IPTV legal?

IPTV is a technology, not a service — and the technology is completely legal. Netflix, YouTube TV, Amazon Prime and Disney+ all use IPTV to deliver their content. The legality question is about whether a specific IPTV service has licensed the content it streams. Licensed services like Mojo 4K are legal to subscribe to and use.

Is Mojo 4K a legal IPTV service?

Mojo 4K operates as a licensed IPTV provider and has been doing so since 2020 with over 100,000 active subscribers. Our six-year uninterrupted track record, public-facing brand, transparent pricing, and open free trial are all consistent with legitimate licensed operation.

How do I tell if an IPTV service is legal?

Signs of a legitimate IPTV service: identifiable company name and website, operating history of multiple years, public pricing and terms, responsive support team, and a free trial. Signs of an illegal (pirate) IPTV service: anonymous operators, extremely cheap pricing with no accountability, no operating history, and no support infrastructure.

Can I get in trouble for using IPTV?

Using a licensed IPTV service like Mojo 4K carries no legal risk — it is equivalent to subscribing to Netflix or Hulu. Using an unlicensed pirate IPTV service may carry subscriber risk in some jurisdictions, though enforcement has primarily targeted providers. The simplest way to eliminate any concern is to use a licensed service.
