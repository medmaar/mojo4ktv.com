Smart STB: a MAG-Style IPTV Box Inside Your TV

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Smart STB is a Samsung and LG TV app that imitates a MAG set-top box. Instead of an M3U playlist it connects to a provider's portal URL and identifies your TV by a MAC address. If you like the classic MAG interface, or your provider is portal-first, it is one of the few ways to get it without buying a box.

How Smart STB Works

A MAG box logs in to a "Stalker" middleware portal using its MAC address. Smart STB does the same in software, so your provider has to link the MAC shown in the app to your subscription. That is the main difference from playlist apps such as SET IPTV: nothing is uploaded by you, the provider authorises the device.

  • Runs on: Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs where the app is listed.
  • Needs: a portal URL and MAC registration from your provider. Mojo 4K provides a MAG portal for exactly this.
  • Cost: Smart STB normally has a short trial and then a paid licence from the developer, separate from your IPTV plan.

Smart STB Setup Step by Step

  1. Install Smart STB from your TV's app store and open it.
  2. Write down the MAC address shown on the start screen.
  3. Send the MAC address to Mojo 4K support on WhatsApp so it can be linked to your plan or trial.
  4. In Smart STB, open Settings > Portal settings and enter the portal URL you receive.
  5. Save and restart the app. The MAG-style menu with Live TV, VOD and the guide appears.

Fixing "Portal Not Available" and Other Errors

  • "STB blocked" or "not authorised": the MAC has not been linked yet. Confirm with support that the exact MAC shown in the app is registered.
  • "Portal not available": check the portal URL for a missing /c/ or trailing slash, then restart.
  • Licence expired: this is the Smart STB app licence, not your IPTV plan. Renew it with the developer.
