IPTV and Canadian Internet Providers

Mojo 4K works with all major Canadian internet providers: Bell, Rogers, Shaw (now Rogers), Telus, Videotron, Cogeco, Eastlink, and SaskTel. Any Canadian broadband connection above 25 Mbps supports stable 4K IPTV streaming. Fibre internet connections from Bell Fibe, Rogers Ignite, or Telus PureFibre easily handle 4K streams with no buffering. Users on slower DSL connections (10–25 Mbps) should set the stream quality to HD 1080p in their IPTV player settings.

Some Canadian ISPs practice traffic shaping during peak hours. If you experience buffering between 7–11 PM, contact your ISP about their traffic management policies. Alternatively, a VPN (NordVPN or ExpressVPN) can route around ISP throttling — though this adds latency, so choose a VPN server geographically close to your location.
