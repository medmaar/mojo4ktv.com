TiviMate Account IPTV — Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a TiviMate account?
TiviMate doesn't require a separate account. The free version works without any login. TiviMate Premium is purchased via Google Play and tied to your Google account — no new account to create.
How many devices can I use with one TiviMate account?
TiviMate Premium purchased via Google Play works on all Android and Firestick devices signed into the same Google account. For multiple devices with separate Google accounts, each needs its own Premium purchase.
I lost my TiviMate Premium — how do I restore it?
Open TiviMate, go to Settings > TiviMate Premium and tap 'Restore Purchase'. Sign in with the same Google account used to purchase. If you changed devices, restore from the same Google Play account.
Can I share my Mojo 4K account with multiple TiviMate devices?
Yes — each Mojo 4K plan includes a set number of simultaneous connections. A 2-device plan lets you stream on 2 devices at once, each running TiviMate with the same credentials.
